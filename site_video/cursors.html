The formula

  • Time: Fitts's law, T = 0.12 + 0.11 · log2(distance / size + 1) s, with ±10% variety, kept between 0.35 and 1.3 s. Far or small targets take longer.
  • Speed: minimum-jerk, s(k) = 10k³ − 15k⁴ + 6k⁵: the smooth start and stop of a real arm.
  • Path: a quadratic Bezier bent sideways by 4 to 12% of the distance, to a random side (the wrist pivots, so moves arc).
  • Wobble: two slow sine waves, at most about 2 px, fading out at both ends.
  • Overshoot: past 300 px it lands 1.5 to 3% beyond the target, then a short correction back.
  • Landing: somewhere near the middle of the button, not on its exact center.
  • Auto: an arrow only in scenes that point at, click or type into something; a scene that only scrolls has none.
